Orbita: Orbital Mechanics

Fly a rocket and discover how orbits really work.

You control the engine and the direction of a rocket that obeys Earth's real gravity. No rails: the trajectory line shows, live, where it is going.

  • Why an orbit is a fall that never reaches the ground
  • Apogee, perigee and escape velocity
  • A Moon rendezvous and a trip to Mars with the Hohmann transfer
  • 23 stages, from training to an expedition across the solar system

Photon Lab: Quantum Puzzles

Mirror-and-light puzzles that teach real quantum physics.

Drag mirrors, beam splitters, filters and detectors, and fire photons one at a time. The rules are a real quantum simulation: you see the phenomenon before you read the explanation.

  • A photon that takes two paths at once
  • Interference, measurement and the bomb found without touching it
  • Tunneling and entangled twin photons
  • 42 levels in 9 chapters, no formulas, ages 8+

Laboratório Periódico

A chemistry RPG where the heroes are elements of the periodic table. Portuguese only.

Build a team of three elements and face villains like the Rust in turn-based battles. To attack, combine two heroes: if the reaction really exists, it happens on screen with the real, balanced equation. If the combination has no source yet, the game says so instead of inventing chemistry.

  • 40 real chemical reactions, each with a recorded source (IUPAC, PubChem, NIST and general chemistry textbooks)
  • The periodic table is the team-selection screen: group, period and atomic number show up as you play
  • Real properties become strategy: aluminum protects itself with a layer of alumina, and chromium and nickel resist corrosion
  • 4 islands and 20 levels: Metals, Salts, Acids and Bases, and Alloys, with a boss at the end of each island

Made for the classroom, too.

Real science

The games use real laws, values and reactions. Where something is simplified to fit the game, the game itself says so, without pretending.

No sign-up, no purchases

Just install and play. Progress stays on the student's device; no account data is requested.

Works offline

Everything runs without internet. Some games show an ad now and then, always between levels and never in the middle of play. Laboratório Periódico has no ads.

Safe for school

The games explain the phenomenon but never teach lab procedures. Dangerous reactions come with a warning.
